How to play

Choose your team

Quick start. Pick a character and begin the trail. Choose a fight, select a rival, play your cards, then end your turn.

Choose Single player for one character, or Share a screen for two. To play on separate screens, find a public match or share a private room code.

Pick a cousin or a woodland familiar. Each cousin also chooses a recruited animal companion. Each animal has a special ability it can use for free once per turn, using its own type. Other woodland creatures can be recruited as you play.

Build a deck

Each character starts with 15 cards. Everyone shares 12 basics, including Claw Strike and Hold Fast. The other three cards suit your character, including their signature moves.

In single player, open Review starting deck to see your cards. Choose Begin the trail when you’re ready. You can change your deck during the run through rewards, merchants, and camps.

Choose a path

Pick a fight on the trail map. Hard fights pay more gold. Win the level 3 Hard fight to choose an extra perk for the rest of the run.

Merchants sell cards and remove unwanted cards for 30 gold. At camp, each character can heal 30% of maximum health, upgrade a card, or remove a card for 30 gold. Trials offer new rival tactics and a featured draft card. These stops and routes are optional.

Play a turn

The rival cards show who you are fighting. Look for Next move to see who they’ll hit or how they’ll defend.

Drag a targeted move onto a rival, or tap the move and then the rival. Tap a Block card to play it immediately, or drag it onto your familiar. Tap other untargeted moves to play them. With a keyboard, press Enter on a targeted move, then use Tab and Enter to choose a rival. A companion’s move is free once per round. When you play solo as an animal, your own ability is also free once per round.

Each round, draw 5 cards and usually start with 3 energy. A card’s number is its cost. An X card spends all remaining energy and repeats once per energy spent. Choose End turn when you’re ready.

In co-op, both players take a turn before the rivals act. Have your partner attack the same rival you hit most recently to trigger a Bond Strike once per round.

Types and effects

Ember beats Grove · Grove beats Tide · Tide beats Stone · Stone beats Ember. A strong matchup deals 50% more damage. The reverse matchup deals 25% less.

Hover, focus, or tap a next move or effect such as Frail for details. For the full list, open Battle info → Combat terms.

Grow and save your run

After a trainer fight, choose a perk for the run. Take a card or select Skip card, then claim your level-up reward. Defeating rivals also unlocks cards and creatures for future runs.

Choose Save game during a run, then Continue saved game on the opening screen. Saves stay in this browser. If you save during a solo reward, claiming it updates that save. A saved room game resumes on one shared screen.

Clear three acts of 20 levels each. Defeat each act's boss to continue. A full run is designed for 90–120 minutes, and you can save your progress along the way.
