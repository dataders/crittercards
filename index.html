How to play

  1. Pick one creature each and build a starting deck. Your creature brings two signature cards. On a shared screen, take turns with the controls.
  2. Spend energy to play cards. Select an enemy first for targeted cards. Enemies act after both players finish their turns.
  3. Fixed card costs range from 0 to 3 energy. An X-cost card spends all remaining energy and repeats its effect once per energy spent.
  4. Hit the enemy your partner attacked most recently to trigger a Bond Strike once per round. Read enemy intents to decide who needs protection.
  5. Strength adds attack damage for the fight. Vulnerable increases incoming damage by 50% on the next hit. Frail reduces the next Block gain by 3.
  6. Defeat rivals to unlock library cards for future decks. After a trainer win, draft a card and pick a level-up perk for the current run.
  7. Pick a route at each level. Tougher fights pay more gold. Spend the shared gold at optional merchants to add cards to either deck for this run.
