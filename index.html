How to play

  1. Pick a cousin or a woodland familiar and build a starting deck. Your character brings two signature cards. On a shared screen, take turns with the controls.
  2. In single player, choose one character. Cards that help an ally help your own character instead, and rivals have less health and attack damage.
  3. Spend energy to play cards. Select an enemy first for targeted cards. Enemies act after both players finish their turns.
  4. Fixed card costs range from 0 to 3 energy. An X-cost card spends all remaining energy and repeats its effect once per energy spent.
  5. Hit the enemy your partner attacked most recently to trigger a Bond Strike once per round. Read enemy intents to decide who needs protection.
  6. Strength adds attack damage for the fight. Vulnerable increases incoming damage by 50% on the next hit. Frail reduces the next Block gain by 3.
  7. Each character has a type. Ember beats Grove, Grove beats Tide, Tide beats Stone, and Stone beats Ember. Super effective attacks deal 50% more damage. Attacking in the reverse direction deals 25% less. Other matchups deal normal damage. Type changes damage before Block and Vulnerable.
  8. Defeat rivals to unlock library cards for future decks. After a trainer win, draft a card and pick a level-up perk for the current run.
  9. Pick a route at each level. Tougher fights pay more gold. Spend the shared gold at optional merchants to add cards to either deck for this run.
  10. At an optional camp, each familiar chooses to heal 30% of its maximum health or upgrade one card for the rest of the run.
  11. Use Save game during a run, then Continue saved game on the opening screen. The save stays in this browser. A room game resumes with both players on one screen.
